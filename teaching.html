Aleksandar Trklja

Teaching & Activities

Teaching, editorial work, and academic service.

Teaching

My teaching covers corpus linguistics, translation technologies, AI and machine learning in the social sciences, research methods, and theoretical approaches to translation.

Curriculum development

I have been involved in the development and revision of university curricula, including BA and MA programmes at the University of Innsbruck, and have served as an international expert in the accreditation of master’s programmes.

Editorial activities

Founding co-editor of Philosophy of Translation (Taylor & Francis). Member of the editorial board of AI & SOCIETY (Springer Nature) and the advisory board of SKASE Journal of Translation and Interpretation.

Selected journal reviewing

Journal of Pragmatics, Corpora, International Journal of Corpus Linguistics, AI & SOCIETY, Scientometrics, Perspectives, Quality & Quantity, International Journal of Data Science and Analytics, and Humanities and Social Sciences Communications.
